## 1.2.0

Version 1.2.0 moves the viewer to api-smart-diff 2.x: OpenAPI 3.1 and 3.2, AsyncAPI 3.0, GraphApi 1.0 and ProtoApi documents, the `risky` and `deprecated` change types, `error` events for unsupported documents and comparison problems in the `ready` event. See [Migration from 1.x](/guide/migration) for what changes for existing users.
